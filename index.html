About Us

A calm corner
by the Baler coast

Wahana Cafe opened its doors in 2023 with one simple idea — a quiet, welcoming room in Baler where good food, better coffee and easy conversation could slow the whole day down. Warm lighting, wooden textures and plenty of greenery do the rest.

Locals and travelers made it their own fast — sunrise coffees before the surf, rainy-day pasta, sunset craft beers and everything in between. Whether you're working, catching up, or simply watching the light change over the water, there's a seat waiting for you.

Good to Know

Before you come by

Walk-ins are always welcome. For groups of 8+ or special celebrations, message us on Facebook a day or two ahead and we'll set a comfortable corner aside for you.
Rest days. They let our small team recharge, deep-clean, and restock fresh bakes — so Thursday through Monday always feel like an opening day. See you after the break, 11:00 AM sharp.
Very. Quiet corners, reliable Wi-Fi and bottomless coffee make us a favorite remote-work spot. Outlets are limited, so grab a seat near the wall if you plan to stay a while.
Street parking is limited and the entry road narrows where tricycles park — a note one of our guests shared. On weekends, we recommend commuting or hopping a short trike ride in from town proper.
Yes — we accept cards, GCash and cash. Contactless is easiest when we're busy at lunch; just flash the QR at the counter when you're ready to settle.
